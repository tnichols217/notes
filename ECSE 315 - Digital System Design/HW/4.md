## 4

For your divider, identify the path the exhibit the worst case delay? (Explain)

> [!answer]
> Likely from $M_{3}$ to $R_{3}$ as it has to make its way rightwards then leftwards through each CAS on each row before propagating downwards through the $Q_{i}$ outputs. Once at the bottom row, it passes through one remainder correction before obtaining $R_{3}$. Since the signal first passes right before propagating left on each row of CAS's, the leftmost CAS will have the most delay out of the row.
