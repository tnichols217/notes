## 3

A CMOS inverter is designed with $\beta_{n} = 2.5\beta_{p}$, $V_{tn}= +1~\text{V}$, and $V_{tp}= -0.9~\text{V}$. Compute $V_{IL}$, $V_{IH}$, and $V_{th}$ if $V_{dd} = 5~\text{V}$.

> [!answer]
> NMOS: $V_{GSn}=V_{in}$, $V_{DSn}=V_{out}$
> PMOS: $V_{GSp}=V_{in}-V_{dd}$, $V_{DSp}=V_{out}-V_{dd}$
> 
> Both NMOS and PMOS are saturated at the switching threshold.
> 
> $\beta_{n}(V_{th}-V_{tn})^{2}=\beta_{p}(V_{th}-V_{dd}-V_{tp})^{2}$
> $\sqrt{2.5\beta_{p}}(V_{th}-V_{tn})=\sqrt{\beta_{p}}(V_{dd}+V_{tp}-V_{th})$
> $V_{th}\sqrt{2.5\beta_{p}}-V_{tn}\sqrt{2.5\beta_{p}}=V_{dd}\sqrt{\beta_{p}}+V_{tp}\sqrt{\beta_{p}}-V_{th}\sqrt{\beta_{p}}$
> $V_{th}\sqrt{2.5\beta_{p}}+V_{th}\sqrt{\beta_{p}}=V_{dd}\sqrt{\beta_{p}}+V_{tp}\sqrt{\beta_{p}}+V_{tn}\sqrt{2.5\beta_{p}}$
> $V_{th}=\frac{\sqrt{\beta_{p}}(V_{dd}+V_{tp})+V_{tn}\sqrt{2.5\beta_{p}}}{\sqrt{2.5\beta_{p}}+\sqrt{\beta_{p}}}$
> $V_{th}=\frac{V_{dd}+V_{tp}+\sqrt{2.5}V_{tn}}{\sqrt{2.5}+1}$
> $=2.20102024884066~\text{V}$
> 
> For $V_{IL}$ and $V_{IH}$ I utilized a linear solver to not need to do the algebra myself.
> 
> ![[8/tables#^3]]
> 
> $V_{IL}=1.72002609818140~\text{V}$
> $V_{IH}=2.47763742627852~\text{V}$
