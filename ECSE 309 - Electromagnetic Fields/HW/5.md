# Problem Set 5

## 1

The smart toilet’s bidet has a micro-spray feature that sweeps a jet of water to help clean things up. The mechanism has sub-millimeter precision over the spray pattern owing to a micromachined Lorentz force actuator. The actuator is held in place by springs, and an equivalent model is shown for your analysis

### a

Assume the springs are ideal and identical and support a $1~\text{mm}$ horizontal wire with a mass of $10~\text{mg}$. The wire is energized with $100~\text{mA}$ of current ($i_{x}$). In the absence of a magnetic field the wire stretches the springs by $20~\micro\text{m}$ in the $-F_{z}$ direction due to gravity. When a maximum strength magnetic field is applied the springs stretch an additional $200~\micro\text{m}$ in the $-F_{z}$ direction. What is the value and direction of the magnetic flux density $B$? Assume $B$ is uniform across the wire and exerts no force on the springs.

Hint: the force of a spring is $F_{z} = k*d$ where the spring constant $k$ is assumed to be constant and $d$ is the stretch distance.

> [!answer]
> $F_{z}=kd$
> $d=20~\micro\text{m}$
> $g=9.8~\text{m/s}$
> $m=10~\text{mg}=10^{-5}~\text{kg}$
> $F_{g}=mg=9.8\times10^{-5}~\text{N}$
> $F_{g}=F_{z}=kd$
> $k=\frac{F_{g}}{d}=\frac{9.8}{2}=4.9~\text{N/m}$
> 
> $F_{B}=kd$
> $d=200~\micro\text{m}$
> $F_{B}=9.8\times 10^{-4}~\text{N}$
> 
> $F_{B}=IL\times B\sin(\theta)$
> $I=100~\text{mA}=0.1~\text{A}$
> $L=1~\text{mm}=0.001~\text{m}$
> $B=\frac{F_{B}}{IL}=\frac{9.8}{0.1(0.001)}\times 10^{-4}=9.8~\text{T}$
> 
> Since $F_{B}$ is in the $-\hat z$ direction, the current is in the $+\hat x$ direction, $B$ should point in the $-\hat y$ direction as $F=L\times B$.
> 
> $B=-9.8\hat y~\text{T}$

## 2

A 20-turn rectangular coil with sides $l = 30~\text{cm}$ and
$w = 10~\text{cm}$ is placed in the $y-z$ plane as shown in Fig. P5.6.

### a

If the coil, which carries a current $I = 10~\text{A}$, is in the presence of a magnetic flux density

$\vec B = 2 \times 10^{-2}(\hat x + \hat y2)~(T)$.

Determine the torque acting on the coil.

> [!answer]
> $\tau=m\times B$
> $m=NIA$
> $N=20$
> $I=10~\text{A}$
> $A=3\times10^{-2}(\pm\hat x)~\text{m}^{2}$
> $m=6~\text{Am}^{2}$
> $\tau=\pm0.06\hat z$

### b

At what angle $\phi$ is the torque zero?

> [!answer]
> $B=a(\hat x+\hat y2)$
> $m\times B=0$
> $m=b(\hat x+2\hat y)$
> $\phi=\arctan(2, 1)=1.1071487177940904$

### c

At what angle $\phi$ is the torque maximum? Determine its value.

> [!answer]
> $B=a(\hat x+\hat y2)$
> $m=b(-2\hat x+\hat y)$
> $\phi=\arctan(1, -2)=2.677945044588987$

## 3

An infinitely long wire carrying a $25~\text{A}$ current in the positive $x$ direction is placed along the $x$ axis in the vicinity of a 20-turn circular loop located in the $x-y$ plane (Fig. P5.11). If the magnetic field at the center of the loop is zero, what is the direction and magnitude of the current ﬂowing in the loop?

> [!answer]
> $B_{wire}=\frac{\micro_{0}I_{wire}}{2\pi d}$
> $=\frac{\mu_{0}}{\pi}\frac{25}{4}~\text{T}$
> $B_{loop}=\frac{N\mu_{0}I_{loop}}{2R}$
> $=\mu_{0}10I~\text{T}$
> $I=\frac{25}{40\pi}$ clockwise from the viewpoint

## 4

In a certain conducting region, the magnetic ﬁeld is given in cylindrical coordinates by

$\vec H=\hat\phi\frac{4}{r}[1-(1+3r)e^{-3r}]$

Find the current density $\vec J$.

> [!answer]
> $J=\nabla\times H$
> 
> $\frac{d\vec Hr}{dr}=12e^{-3r}-12e^{-3r}+36re^{-3r}=36re^{-3r}$
> 
> $J=36re^{-3r}\hat z~\text{A/m}^{2}$

## 5

A thin current element extending between $z = -L/2$ and $z = L/2$ carries a current $I$ along $+\hat z$ through a circular cross-section of radius $a$.

### a

Find $\vec A$ at a point $P$ located very far from the origin (assume $R$ is so much larger than $L$ that point $P$ may be considered to be at approximately the same distance from every point along the current element).

> [!answer]
> $\frac{\mu_{0}}{4\pi}\int \frac{I}{R}~dl$
> $=\frac{\mu_{0}IL}{4\pi R}~\hat z$

### b

Determine the corresponding $\vec H$.

> [!answer]
> $H=\nabla\times \frac{A}{\mu_{0}}$
> $A=\frac{\mu_{0}IL}{4\pi R}~(\hat R\cos\theta-\hat\theta\sin\theta)$
> $H=\hat\phi\frac{IL\sin\theta}{4\pi R^{2}}~\text{A/m}$
