## 7.4.1

Suppose the heap consists of seven chunks starting at address $0$. The sizes of the chunks in order are $80~30~60~50~70~20~40$ bytes. When we place an object in a chunk we put it at the high end if there is enough space remaining to form a smaller chunk so that the smaller chunk can easily remain on the linked list of free space. However we cannot tolerate chunks of fewer than $8$ bytes so if an object is almost as large as the selected chunk, we give it the entire chunk and place the object at the low end of the chunk. If we request space for objects of the following sized $32~64~48~16$ in that order what does the free space list look like after satisfying the requests if the method of selecting chunks is

```
80, 30, 60, 50, 70, 20, 40

First fit:
32 -> 48 (32) 30 60 50 70 20 40
64 -> 48 (32) 30 60 50 (64 + 6) 20 40
48 -> (48) (32) 30 60 50 (64 + 6) 20 40
16 -> (48) (32) 14 (16) 60 50 (64 + 6) 20 40

Best fit:
32 -> 80 30 60 50 70 20 8 (32)
64 -> 80 30 60 50 8 (64) 20 8 (32)
48 -> 80 30 60 (48 + 2) 8 (64) 20 8 (32)
16 -> 80 30 60 (48 + 2) 8 (64) 8 (16) 8 (32)
```

> [!answer]

>
