## 3.9

Let $X$ be continuous random variable with destiny function

$f(x)=\begin{cases}3e^{-3x}&x>0\\0&\text{else}\end{cases}$

### b

Compute $E[e^{2X}]$

> [!answer]
> $Y=e^{2X}$
> $X=\frac{\ln(Y)}{2}$
> $\frac{dX}{dY}=\frac{1}{2Y}$
> 
> $f_{Y}(y)=f\left( \frac{\ln(y)}{2} \right)| \frac{1}{2y}|\quad :y>1$
> $f_{Y}(y)=\frac{3}{2}y^{-\frac{5}{2}}\quad :y>1$
> 
> $E[Y]=\int\limits_{1}^{\infty} \frac{3}{2}y^{-3/2}~dy$
> $=-3y^{-1/2}\bigg|_{1}^\infty$
> $=3$

## 3.14

Find the variance of the random variable $X$

### a

For $X$ with the density function

$f(x)=\begin{cases}3e^{-3x}&x>0\\0&\text{else}\end{cases}$

> [!answer]
> We could treat this like an exponential, much like the previous problem, but I will work through the steps manually for practice.
> 
> $E[X]=\int\limits_{0}^{\infty}3xe^{-3x}~dx$
> $=-(x+\frac{1}{3})e^{-3x}\bigg|_{0}^{\infty}$
> $=\frac{1}{3}$
> 
> $Y=X^{2}$
> $X=\sqrt Y$
> $\frac{dX}{dY}=\frac{1}{2\sqrt Y}$
> 
> $f_{Y}(y)=\frac{f(\sqrt y)}{2\sqrt y}\quad:y>0$
> $=\frac{3e^{-3\sqrt y}}{2\sqrt y}\quad:y>0$
> 
> $E[Y]=\int\limits_{0}^{\infty} \frac{3}{2}\sqrt ye^{-3\sqrt y}$
> $=\frac{2}{9}$
> 
> $\text{Var}(X)=E[X^{2}]-E[X]^{2}$
> $\frac{2}{9}- \frac{1}{9}=\frac{1}{9}$

### b

For $X$ with domain $\{1,2,3,4,5\}$ and PMF.

| $x$             | 1     | 2      | 3       | 4      | 5      |
| --------------- | ----- | ------ | ------- | ------ | ------ |
| $p_{X}(x)$      | $1/7$ | $1/14$ | $3/14$  | $2/7$  | $2/7$  |
| $xp_{X}(x)$     | $1/7$ | $1/7$  | $9/14$  | $8/7$  | $10/7$ |
| $x^{2}p_{X}(x)$ | $1/7$ | $2/7$  | $27/14$ | $32/7$ | $50/7$ |

> [!answer]
> $\sum\limits_{n=1}^{5}x_{n}p_{X}(x_{n})=\frac{49}{14}$
> $\sum\limits_{n=1}^{5}x_{n}^{2}p_{X}(x_{n})=\frac{197}{14}$
> 
> $\text{Var}(X)=E[X^{2}]-E[X]^{2}$
> $=\frac{357}{196}=\frac{51}{28}$

## 3.68

Let $Z\sim\mathscr N(0, 1)$ and $X\sim\mathscr N(\mu,\sigma^{2})$

### a

Calculate $E[Z^{4}]$

> [!answer]
> By using our solution for part b, we produce:

> $E[Z^{4}]=3$

### b

Calculate $E[X^{4}]$

> [!answer]
> $M(t)=e^{\mu t+\sigma^2t^2/2}$
> 
> By utilizing the MGF
> 
> $M^{(4)}(t)=e^{\mu t+(\sigma^{2}t^{2})/2}(3\sigma^{4}+6\sigma^{2}(\mu+\sigma^{2}t)^{2}+(\mu+\sigma^{2}t)^{4})$
