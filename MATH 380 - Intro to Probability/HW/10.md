## 7.4

Suppose that $X$ and $Y$ are independent exponential random variables with parameters $\lambda\ne\mu$. Find the density function of $X+Y$

> [!answer]
> $\int\limits_{0}^{s}\lambda e^{-\lambda x}\mu e^{-\mu(s-x)}~dx=\lambda\mu e^{-\mu s}\frac{1-e^{(\mu - \lambda)s}}{\lambda-\mu}=\frac{\lambda\mu}{\lambda-\mu}(e^{-\mu s}-e^{-\lambda s})$

## 8.2

Caleb has a four-sided die, a six-sided die, and a 12-sided die. He rolls the three dice and adds up the numbers showing. What is the expected value of the sum of the rolls?

> [!answer]
> $A\sim\text{Uniform}(1, 4)$
> $B\sim\text{Uniform}(1, 6)$
> $C\sim\text{Uniform}(1, 12)$
> $S=A+B+C$
> 
> $E[S]=E[A+B+C]=E[A]+E[B]+E[C]$
> $=2.5+3.5+6.5=12.5$

## 8.8

Our faucet is broken, and a plumber has been called. The arrival time of the plumber is uniformly distributed between 1PM and 7PM. Independently of when the plumber arrives, the time it takes to fix the broken faucet is exponentially distributed with mean $30$ minutes. Find the expectation and variance of the time at which the plumber completes the project.

> [!answer]
> $A\sim\text{Uniform}(1, 7)$
> $T\sim\text{Exp}(\beta=0.5)$
> $C=A+T$
> $E[C]=E[A+T]=E[A]+E[T]$
> $=4+0.5=\text{4:30 PM}$
